Privacy Policy for MyBulk

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

1. Introduction

MyBulk is developed and operated by Adrián Attila Szép ("we", "us"), acting as the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). MyBulk is a freemium app: the base features are provided at no cost, with optional subscription-based features available. This page explains what information the app handles, why, and what your rights are.

2. What We Collect

MyBulk has no user accounts and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address or any other directly identifying information, and we do not build profiles of individual users.

Your meal logs, recipes, calorie and protein history, weight entries and app settings are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to us and we have no ability to read them.

Limited technical data is unavoidably processed when you use the optional AI Meal Planner, and limited diagnostic data may reach us through Apple. Both are described in Sections 5 and 6.

3. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

MyBulk offers subscription-based paid features. We do not collect or store your payment information, such as card numbers or billing addresses. All payments, subscriptions and free trials are handled entirely by Apple:

  • Apple App Store: Privacy Policy

We receive only aggregated, anonymised sales and subscription reporting from Apple. This does not identify individual purchasers.

4. Apple Health

MyBulk can optionally read your body weight from Apple Health, only if you explicitly grant permission when prompted. This permission is read-only and entirely optional; the app works fully without it.

Weight data imported from Apple Health is stored on your device only. It is never transmitted to us, never sent to any third party, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold. It is not included in anything sent to the AI Meal Planner services described below.

You can revoke access at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Privacy & Security → Health → MyBulk.

5. AI Meal Planner (Pro feature)

If you use the optional AI Meal Planner, a limited snapshot of data is sent to the following third parties solely to generate your meal plan:

  • OpenAI, L.L.C.: Receives the names, categories, calorie and protein values, prep times and caffeine flags of the recipes in your library, along with your calculated daily calorie and protein targets, your meal times and the number of meals you plan per day, in order to generate a suggested meal plan. OpenAI does not receive recipe photos, ingredients, instructions, your weight, height, activity level, or any Apple Health data. Our OpenAI account has data sharing disabled, which means these inputs and outputs are not used to train or improve OpenAI's models. OpenAI may retain API data for a limited period for abuse monitoring in line with its own policies. See OpenAI's API data usage policy and Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: Operates the server that relays this request to OpenAI and independently verifies your subscription with Apple. Cloudflare processes your device's IP address to prevent abuse of this feature. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

We do not store the contents of these requests. We keep only a short-lived usage counter, automatically deleted within 48 hours, used to prevent abuse of the feature.

If you never use the AI Meal Planner, none of your data leaves your device through this route.

6. Diagnostic Data

If you have chosen to share analytics and crash data with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide us with crash reports and performance statistics. Apple aggregates and anonymises this information before we see it, and we cannot use it to identify you. You can turn this off at any time in the iOS Settings app, under Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Separately, as noted above, Cloudflare processes your IP address when you use the AI Meal Planner. We do not operate any other analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs in MyBulk.

7. Legal Basis for Processing (EU/UK users)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) - processing the recipe and target data needed to generate the meal plan you have requested, and verifying your subscription entitlement.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) - processing IP addresses and a short-lived usage counter to prevent abuse of a paid feature, and using anonymised crash data to keep the app working.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) - reading weight from Apple Health, which happens only if you grant permission and can be withdrawn at any time.

8. Security

We value your trust and use commercially reasonable means to protect the limited data we handle. Requests to the AI Meal Planner are transmitted over encrypted connections. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Links to Other Sites

This Service may contain links to other sites. If you click a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. These external sites are not operated by us, and we strongly advise you to review their privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

MyBulk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process personal information from anyone under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, for instance by writing to our support email, we delete it from our records immediately.

Some countries set a higher age at which a person can consent to their own data being processed. Under GDPR Article 8 this digital consent age is between 13 and 16 depending on the country, and in Hungary it is 16. If you are below the digital consent age where you live, please ask a parent or guardian to read this policy and consent on your behalf before using the optional AI Meal Planner or the Apple Health import. All other features of MyBulk keep your data on your own device.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take the necessary action.

11. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location (including the EU, the UK and California), you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of data we hold about you, and to data portability.

In practice, MyBulk stores your meal, recipe, weight and calorie data locally on your device, so you can erase effectively all of your data at any time by deleting the app. We hold no copy of it.

The only data that leaves your device is described in Section 5, and we retain none of its contents beyond the 48-hour abuse counter. OpenAI and Cloudflare retain data only in accordance with their own policies, linked above.

To exercise any right, or if you have a complaint, contact us at the address in Section 13. EU users also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Hungary this is the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), naih.hu.
