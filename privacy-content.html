Adrián Attila Szép built the MyBulk app as a Freemium app. This service is provided at no cost for the base features, with optional subscription-based features available. This page is used to inform visitors regarding policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decides to use this Service.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not directly collect, harvest, or store any personal data that can be used to directly identify or contact you. However, to provide a seamless experience, the app utilizes third-party services and generates technical log data (such as device info and IP addresses) as detailed below.

3. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

MyBulk offers subscription-based paid features. We do not directly collect or store your payment information, such as credit card numbers or billing addresses. All payments and subscriptions are handled securely by third-party payment processors. These third parties have their own privacy policies and terms of service regarding how they handle your financial information:

  • Apple App Store: Privacy Policy

4. Third-Party Services

The app relies on standard Apple developer services (such as App Store Connect for crash reporting and subscription processing) to function correctly. These services may collect technical identifiers in accordance with Apple's privacy standards.

5. Log Data

Whenever you use our Service, in a case of an error in the app, we collect data and information (through third-party products) on your phone called Log Data. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

6. Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

7. Links to Other Sites

This Service may contain links to other sites. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us. Therefore, we strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of these websites.

8. Children's Privacy

In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information (for instance, via a support email), we immediately delete this from our records. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to do necessary actions.

9. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location (such as the EU or California), you have the right to access, update, or delete the data we hold about you. Because MyBulk processes and stores your meal and calorie data locally on your device, you can completely erase your data at any time simply by deleting the app from your device.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
