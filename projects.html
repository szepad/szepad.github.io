Rogue Melee

Rogue Melee is an action roguelike where you fight your way through a randomly generated dungeon. As you descend deeper, every floor has a unique theme, cast of enemies, bosses and treasure to find. You will also acquire stronger weapons and items that empower you on your journey.

Technical Details

Developed in C# using the Raylib framework.

  • Over 35,000 lines of code across 379 files
  • Clean and concise codebase
  • 2D real-time lighting with GLSL shaders
  • Efficient and precise collision detection
  • Custom pathfinding
